Reproducing
-----------
  bash test_run/run_test.sh

Both stages are deterministic for a fixed seed, so on this platform the output
is byte-identical to the files in this directory. run_test.sh nevertheless
grades numerically (exact muon count, survival fraction within a quarter of its
binomial sigma, mean exit energy within 0.5%), because a different gfortran or
libm can legitimately change the last bits of a double without anything being
wrong.
