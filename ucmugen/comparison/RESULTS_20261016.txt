UCMuGen vs EcoMug, re-measured 2026-10-16 with UCMuGen 0.3.0 (UCMuon 1.4.0).
Geant4 11.4.1, EcoMug v2.2, Apple Silicon Mac. Same programs and settings as
RESULTS_20260730.txt (UCMuGen 0.2), which this supersedes. Both generators
given the identical differential flux (UCMuGen's Guan parametrisation, handed
to EcoMug via SetDifferentialFlux), p in [1, 1000] GeV/c, theta < 70 deg.
