UCMuGen vs EcoMug, measured 2026-07-30. Geant4 11.4.1, EcoMug v2.2, Apple
Silicon Mac. Both generators given the identical differential flux (UCMuGen's
Guan parametrisation, handed to EcoMug via SetDifferentialFlux), p in
[1, 1000] GeV/c, theta < 70 deg.

== rate_crosscheck ==============================================================
Two independently written rate integrators on the same function.

  horizontal plane   UCMuGen 9.82058e-3 +- 2e-6  cm^-2 s^-1
                     EcoMug  9.82213e-3 +- 5e-6  cm^-2 s^-1
                     -> 1.6e-4 relative, 0.30 sigma

  cylinder, lateral  UCMuGen 224.141 +- 0.112 Hz
                     EcoMug  224.304 +- 0.259 Hz
                     -> 7e-4 relative

  live time for 1e6 muons through a 2 x 2 m plane
                     UCMuGen 2553 s
                     EcoMug  0 s   (GetEstimatedTime exits early for custom J)

EcoMug's value is the mean over 8 seeds at 5e7 points each, with the spread of
the means as its uncertainty. Its estimator samples uniformly in momentum over a
steeply falling spectrum, so a single run at 2e7 points sat 1.9 sigma away; the
per-run quoted error is optimistic.

== cylinder_g4 ==================================================================
1 x 1 x 0.1 m plate at the origin, 8e6 primaries per case, tracked in Geant4
with transportation only through vacuum (the quantity under test is geometric
acceptance).

  case                                  hit frac      rate into plate [Hz]
  0  UCMuGen plane 7x7 m at z=1 m       2.2472%       108.13 +- 0.26
  4  UCMuGen plane 5x5 m at z=0.5 m     4.3918%       107.81 +- 0.18
  1  UCMuGen cylinder r=1.5 h=1, caps   11.7141%      107.56 +- 0.13
  2  UCMuGen same cylinder, caps OFF    0.4229%       0.9478 +- 0.0052
  3  EcoMug  same cylinder (lateral)    0.4352%       0.9761 +- 0.0054

  surface independence:  plane1 vs cylinder +0.53%, plane2 vs cylinder +0.24%,
                         all pairwise differences within 2 sigma
  caps disabled:         factor 113.5 low
  EcoMug:                factor 110.2 low; within 2.98% of the caps-off case,
                         which localises the deficit to the missing caps

The 2.98% residual between EcoMug and the caps-off configuration is real
(3.8 sigma at 8e6, and 2.5% at 2e6) and reflects a small difference in the
angular distribution sampled across the lateral surface. It is not the caps.

== two mistakes made and fixed, recorded so they are not repeated ==============
1. The first reference plane (7x7 m at z=2 m) truncated the acceptance: a 70 deg
   muon travels 2*tan(70) = 5.5 m laterally, beyond the 3.5 m half-width, so the
   "reference" came out BELOW the cylinder. A second plane of different size and
   height was added as an independent check.
2. Only the binomial error on the hit fraction was propagated at first, ignoring
   the Monte Carlo error on the surface rate. That understated the total and
   made a 0.5% agreement look like a 2 sigma discrepancy.
